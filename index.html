    Who lives where

    How it rotates

    • Everyone walks through the first floor, so everyone helps keep it clean. Monday's cleaning and Thursday's mopping each pair one first-floor resident with someone from upstairs.
    • The second and third floor common spaces are cleaned only by the people who live on them.
    • Trash pairs someone from the second floor with someone from the third. The first floor already works its own floor twice a week, so it skips trash.
    • The kitchen gets cleaned every other Sunday by three people, one from each floor.
    • Nobody gets two jobs in the same week. Over ten weeks, everyone ends up with about the same number of jobs, and your partners change.
    • The chart moves to a new week every Sunday on its own.
